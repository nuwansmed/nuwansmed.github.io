    SabraPoems - Android App for Literature Enthusiasts¶

    Overview¶

    SabraPoems was my first Android app, developed in 2019 to support students in the "Appreciation of English Literary Texts" course at the Diploma of English at Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka. The app aimed to provide a convenient resource for students to access and explore a collection of recommended poems and related literature materials. The goal was to enhance the learning experience by centralizing essential poetry resources in a mobile-friendly format.

    The app was built using HTML, CSS, Semantic-UI, JavaScript, and deployed through Apache Cordova for cross-platform compatibility. I hosted the app’s backend services on Heroku and successfully published it on the Google Play Store, where it garnered several tens of downloads.

    Key Technologies¶

    • Frontend: HTML, CSS, Semantic-UI
    • Backend: JavaScript, Heroku for deployment
    • Mobile Deployment: Apache Cordova
    • Design Framework: Semantic-UI for responsive and visually appealing design

    Features¶

    1. Curated Collection of Poems
      The app featured a curated collection of poems recommended for the course. Students could easily browse and read the poems in a clean and organized layout, which was designed to enhance their reading experience.

    2. Links to Important Resources
      SabraPoems included links to external resources, such as critical analyses, literary commentary, and reference materials. This provided users with a comprehensive understanding of each poem’s themes, background, and stylistic elements.

    3. Simple and User-Friendly Interface
      Leveraging Semantic-UI, the app had a modern and minimalist design, making it easy for students to navigate and access the content they needed. The user interface was intentionally kept simple to avoid distractions while reading.

    4. Offline Access (Planned)
      While the initial version required an internet connection for some features, I planned to implement offline capabilities for accessing poems directly within the app without needing continuous connectivity.

    Motivation¶

    The inspiration for SabraPoems came from my own experiences and feedback from peers who faced challenges in accessing literary resources for the course. The idea was to create a mobile app that could serve as a portable literature companion, enabling students to engage with poetry anytime, anywhere. With the increasing use of smartphones among students, an Android app was a natural choice to deliver this convenience.

    Challenges and Learnings¶

    Being my first Android app, SabraPoems was a significant learning experience in mobile app development. Key takeaways included: - Understanding the integration of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with Apache Cordova for cross-platform mobile apps. - Learning to use Semantic-UI for responsive design and a visually appealing layout. - Managing app deployment on Heroku and handling basic backend tasks with JavaScript. - Publishing an app on the Google Play Store and understanding the nuances of app deployment and user feedback.

    Project Status¶

    The app was successfully published on the Google Play Store in 2019 and achieved several tens of downloads. Although the codebase and screenshots are no longer available, SabraPoems remains a memorable milestone in my journey as a developer, reflecting my commitment to creating practical solutions for educational challenges.

    SabraPoems serves as an example of leveraging technology to make learning more accessible and engaging, especially in the realm of literature and poetry.
